Our Craft

Built by hand, shaped by nature

Twisted Grains Woodworking is a small, local woodshop specializing in custom furniture, decor, and one-of-a-kind pieces built from live-edge slabs, burls, and reclaimed wood. We let each piece of wood guide the design, highlighting the natural grain, knots, and curves instead of hiding them.

From statement coffee tables to hand-carved decor, every item that leaves our shop is made right here in Phoenixville — built to be the kind of piece you hand down, not replace.
